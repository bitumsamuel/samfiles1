Frequently Asked Questions

What is Selfless CE?

A non-profit that helps young single adults become self-sufficient through online education with BYU Pathway Worldwide, paired with self-improvement. Education and self-improvement are the keys — SELFLESS really does begin with “self.”

How can I join?

Selfless is open to young single adults ages 18–30 willing to learn and grow through BYU Pathway Worldwide online education and self-improvement. Join by filling out the application form on this site.

What are the benefits of joining?

Tuition support for qualifying individuals, a weekly stipend to help students through the school term, and rewards for outstanding and best-performing students.

What are the registration requirements?

Joining is free. You must be a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, or a close friend of Selfless members, and maintain a high standard of conduct.
